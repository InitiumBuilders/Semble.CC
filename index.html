Yours

    The word

    Semble.

    To come together. Something worth coming together around.

    It carries assemble inside it, but softer and closer — an invitation rather than a summons. A Semble is both the gathering and the reason for it: the thing you bring, and the people who come to meet it.

    Build your Semble. Share it in person with the world.

    The loop

    Six steps, and none of them are posting.

    Everything in Semble is made of these. Nothing else needs learning.

    Step 01 · Keep

    Your Semble

    One active piece of work, shared in the open. Not a bio, not a portfolio — the thing you are actually making right now.

    Step 02 · Init

    A Semble Session

    One gathering, in one real place, at one real time. Open one: what you convene on, where, when, and how many people it needs. Anywhere real — a library, a park, a workshop.

    Step 03 · Commit

    A stake, not an RSVP

    Nothing is scheduled until enough people commit, and committing means putting down a small stake that comes straight back when you walk in. If it never fills, everyone who committed gets back more than they put down, funded by the convener's bond.

    Step 04 · Gather

    Phones down

    Nothing records by default. Anyone present can mark a moment, and only marked moments are kept.

    Step 05 · Sign

    Your Trax, signed by the room

    Everyone taps the Sesh anchor and co-signs for each other. The line your Steps make: every Sesh you attended, dated, signed by the people who were there, and checkable by anyone. No location is captured.

    Step 06 · Credit

    !MOTUS, to what you brought

    A fixed pool per Sesh, allocated by the people who were present. That credit anchors the compute you build with.

    Presence and compute

    Computation flows from showing up.

    Most of the industry is selling agents that work while you sleep. Semble runs the other way: your agent moves while you are there, guiding it — and the capacity to run it is earned by being in rooms with people.

    Credits are counted in supervised agent-minutes, a unit you can reason about. Earned with your feet. Spent while present.

    What presence actually buys you

    Every consequential action an agent takes here is taken with a person present, signed, and logged. That makes it attributable — you can always say who was there and what they authorised — and interruptible — someone is close enough to stop it.

    That is a safety and accountability claim, and it is the one we make.
