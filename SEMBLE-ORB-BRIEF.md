# THE ORB & MAINBOARD — THE STANDING BRIEF
### August's contract for the backend world. Checked on every build. Not re-litigated.

This file exists so the brief never has to be repeated. Every change to `assets/orb.js`
is checked against this list before it ships.

## The world

1. **Real, not computer-generated.** The chips must look like photographs of actual
   modern silicon — heat-spreader lids, HBM stacks on interposers, BGA arrays,
   stiffener frames, substrate layers, test points, barcodes, lot codes, flux
   residue, one consistent light source. Never a diagram, never a "render."
2. **Alien-level technology, black dark mode, cyan accented.** The body is black.
   Light lives only where energy is. Consistent colors — one cold family computes,
   the warm family (gold/amber) ignites. Never cheesy, never rainbow.
3. **Nothing overlaps.** Every component relax-spaced and aligned at every size.
4. **The world scrolls with the reader.** The board is taller than the viewport and
   pans with the page. It is a place, not a backdrop.
5. **A chip for every building block.** Every core word of the platform is silicon:
   CC CORES · TRAX GFX · STEPS · SESH · INIT · CROSSING · !MOTUS PWR · NET ·
   SCU ARRAY · SCC CORES · MCC MOTUS · GUIDE · SEATS · MODELS · COMMONS · THE LOOP.
6. **Readable.** Every silkscreen label crisp and legible. Full resolution, never
   scaled up from half.

## The orb

7. **Glass, futuristic, truly magical.** An instrument, not a bubble. A thin organic
   hexagon lattice filters through it — alien optics viewing a core building block.
8. **It has a mind.** Movement is genuinely random and evolving — never the same tour
   twice, never a ping-pong between two spots, memory of where it has been,
   exploration drifts, varied dwell.
9. **It locks on.** It centers a single component — compute units favored — settles
   dead still, scans it with a reticle, then moves on.
10. **It feeds.** Motes travel orb → chip, the beam fires, the whole chip pulses,
    a shockwave rings, energy circulates the nets. Then it decays and re-arms.
11. **It grows with the journey.** Chip-small at the top of the page, full instrument
    by the bottom — and **the growth curve is organic and different every visit**,
    never the same programmed ramp.
12. **The chips sing.** Music notes rise from components while the energy runs.

## Mobile

13. **The navbar orb is the same engine** — real shader, symbolic, magical, clear,
    each tab its own construct and its own light. Never a sketch of the desktop one.

## The tiers (locked language, everywhere)

- **SCU — Semble Compute Units.** The core building block.
- **SCC — Semble Compute Cores.** Linked SCUs; the working tier.
- **MCC — Motus Compute Cores.** The highest tier. Ignites at thresholds — never bought.
