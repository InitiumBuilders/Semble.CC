The compute rail

Computation flows from presence.

Three tiers. One rule: the top one cannot be bought.

SCU · SEMBLE COMPUTE UNITS

The core building block: one unit of agent capacity, run while you are present. SCUs link — a room's units join into one fabric, and fabrics network across rooms.

SCC · SEMBLE COMPUTE CORES

Cores made of linked SCUs — the working tier. A room runs on its cores; !MOTUS meters what they burn.

MCC · MOTUS COMPUTE CORES

The highest tier. Motus-level compute for the magic moments and thresholds — an MCC ignites when a threshold is crossed, not when it is paid for.
