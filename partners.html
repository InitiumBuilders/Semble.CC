Become a partner

What we look for.

Four things. They are not negotiable, and they are the reason the partnerships that exist actually work.

① Value stays with the maker

Artists, builders, contributors — whoever creates the value should keep the majority of it. If the model needs an intermediary to take 70%, we are not the right partner.

② Claims you can check

We publish numbers that do not flatter us, and we expect the same. A partnership built on two sets of unverifiable claims is a press release, not a build.

③ A real first move

Something a stranger can do in one click, today, that produces a real result. Not a waitlist. Not a signup. A move.

④ Willing to be built with

We bring compute, rails and systems work — that means touching your architecture. The best partnerships are the ones where both sides change.
