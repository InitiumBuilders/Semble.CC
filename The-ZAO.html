Why this partnership

Two projects, one conviction.

The ZAO says the value an artist creates should stay with the artist. Semble says the same thing about the people watching. Put those together and you get something neither has alone: a live room where the audience is not an audience — it is the machine.

What The ZAO fixes

Streaming economics are broken and the numbers are not subtle. Spotify pays roughly $0.003–0.005 per stream. Artists wait 30–90 days to be paid. Platforms and labels take 70–87% before a cent reaches the person who made the music.

The ZAO — ZTalent Artist Organization — exists to “empower independent creators to reclaim profit margins, data, and IP rights.” Members hold ownership in those things instead of surrendering them.

What Semble brings

MotusCompute turns a livestream audience into a verified compute pool. One press, no install, no account, no wallet — a viewer's browser starts doing real work, and gets a receipt they can independently re-verify.

It is open source, the accounting is public, and every claim on the page is falsifiable with curl. We built the ledger before we built the jobs, on purpose.

The product we are building for

WaveWarZ.

“The world's first music prediction market.” Two artists. Three consecutive 20-minute battles. A Triple Judging System — human judges, X community votes, and SOL pool voting — where 2 out of 3 takes the round.

Artist take1%of trading volume, in real time — not in 90 days
Platform fees1.5%against an industry standard north of 30%
Stays in98.5%of every trade remains in the ecosystem
Ready to battle50+curated artists · daily X Spaces, M–F 11am ET

Built on Solana

Individual Program Derived Addresses per battle, supporting unlimited concurrent battles. Rust and Anchor for the programs; React, TypeScript and Solana Web3.js on the front end; Phantom and Solflare for wallets.

The prize split is the part worth reading twice: 50% back to losing traders, 40% to winners, 5% to the winning artist, 3% platform, 2% to the losing artist. Even the losing side of a battle gets paid.

Semble × The ZAO — the build

What a Build Partner
actually does.

Not a logo swap. Semble is committing infrastructure, and the loop closes on a battle night.

  1. 1

    A battle goes live

    Two ZAO artists, twenty minutes, a room full of people already watching and already invested in the outcome.

  2. 2

    The room lends compute

    One press in the browser. WebGPU is probed for real — never guessed — and the machine joins the pool. Closing the tab ends it.

  3. 3

    Strangers verify it

    Every unit is recomputed by 3 independent machines and only counts when they agree. Roughly 1 in 4 is a known-answer probe a client cannot identify.

  4. 4

    Everyone keeps proof

    A receipt naming the unit, the task it fed and a digest anyone can re-run. Paid in $DASH or $TRUST — the contributor chooses.

What Semble is putting in

  • Compute for WaveWarZ. The MotusCompute pool, wired to battle nights — the moment a stream has the most attention is exactly the moment it has the most idle hardware.
  • Open infrastructure. Every rail is public at github.com/InitiumBuilders/SEMBLE — the ledger, the verification, the payout engine, the kernels. Fork it, audit it, break it.
  • Verified accounting. Contribution is attributed per-artist and per-mode, accrued in a public ledger, and settled on rails that publish every batch.
  • Systems design. Loop mapping, leverage analysis and emergent-strategy work on the ZAO ecosystem itself — the thing Semble does before it writes a line of code.
  • A live surface. The MotusLive stage — eleven rendered worlds, a ranked community voice, and a broadcast rail already running at semble.cc/live.

Where this actually is

What is live, and
what is not.

A partnership announcement is a good place to overstate things. We would rather it be the place we do not — the whole value of what Semble builds is that you can check it.

Read from the ledger when you opened this page
—MOTUS-seconds contributed
—units verified by 3 strangers
—machines that have joined
—paid out so far

That last number is the one most pages would leave off. The rail is built and tested end to end; it has not been armed, so nothing has been sent. It changes the day it changes. Run it yourself — curl semble.cc/api/compute

  • Live and verifiable. The compute rail: pledge → dispatch → verify → receipt → accrual. Three-machine consensus, canary probes, a public payout audit log, and an operator bridge. Check it: curl semble.cc/api/work
  • Built and deliberately unarmed. The payout engine, verified end to end including replay-safety. No money has been sent yet — moneyMoved: false is the live proof, and it flips the day that changes.
  • Being built together. The WaveWarZ integration — wiring the pool to battle nights, and attributing contribution to the artists on stage. That work starts now.
  • Not built. Pooled inference of a large model. The INT8 matmul tile is the correct primitive and it works, but a real forward pass needs weight distribution and layer scheduling a pool of browser tabs does not have yet. We will say so until it is true.

The hard part was never the compute. It was trust. So we built the accounting first, made it public, and let anyone check the arithmetic.
